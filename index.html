Frequently Asked Questions

What is PixelSqueeze?

PixelSqueeze is a free online image compressor that reduces JPG, PNG and WebP image sizes directly in your browser.

Is PixelSqueeze free?

Yes. PixelSqueeze is completely free to use with no hidden charges.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Your images stay on your device because all compression happens in your browser.

Which formats are supported?

PixelSqueeze supports JPG, PNG and WebP images.

Does image compression reduce quality?

You can adjust the compression quality to balance image size and visual quality.
