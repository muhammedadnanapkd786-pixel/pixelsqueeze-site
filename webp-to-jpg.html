Frequently asked questions

Is this WebP to JPG converter really free?

Yes. The converter is completely free to use, with no limits on how many images you convert.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every conversion happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device.

Can I convert multiple WebP files to JPG at once?

Yes. You can drag and drop or select multiple WebP files, and each one will be converted and available to download individually or all at once.

What happens to transparency when converting WebP to JPG?

JPG does not support transparency, so any transparent areas in your WebP file are filled with a solid white background during conversion.
