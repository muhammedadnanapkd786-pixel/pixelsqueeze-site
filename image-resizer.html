Frequently asked questions

Is this image resizer really free?

Yes. The resizer is completely free to use, with no limits on how many images you resize.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every resize happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes. You can drag and drop or select multiple JPG, PNG, or WebP files, and each one is resized according to the same settings and available to download individually or all at once.

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Scaling an image down generally keeps it looking sharp. Scaling an image up beyond its original size can make it look softer, since no new detail is being created.

What does locking the aspect ratio do?

With aspect ratio locked, changing the width automatically adjusts the height (and vice versa) so the image is scaled proportionally and never looks stretched or squashed.
