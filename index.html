Frequently asked questions

Is PixelSqueeze really free to use?

Yes. PixelSqueeze is completely free, with no limits on how many images you compress.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every compression happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device.

Which image formats does PixelSqueeze support?

PixelSqueeze compresses JPG, PNG, and WebP images, and can also convert between these formats or resize images.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Yes. You can drag and drop or select multiple images, and each one is compressed with the same quality settings and available to download individually or all at once.
